Welcome to LibSearch.id

A website used to borrow books from the library

Library Rules & Policies

  • An Active Membership is used for borrowing Physical and Digital books.
  • For Standard Access you have to return a book within 14 days or less.
  • You are RESPONSIBLE for your own borrowed books within the library, any damages will be charged on YOUR account.
  • Respect the book, and do NOT damage any borrowed books.

How Membership Works

  1. Registration: Complete the digital application form using verified credentials.
  2. Verification: System validates your application status against institutional records.
  3. Issuance: Receive your unique digital library identification barcode and account profile.
